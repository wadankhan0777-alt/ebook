Folio is free & open source. Every book comes from Project Gutenberg — public-domain literature digitized by volunteers — via the open Gutendex catalog. Narration is generated on your device with the Web Speech API. No account, no tracking, no cost.

📲 Use it like an app, offline: every book you open or download is saved on your device. On Android/desktop Chrome tap “Get app” above (or menu → Install app); on iPhone open in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Your own .epub and .txt books can be added with ＋ Import.
